Quick guide

Three steps to study

Research, organize, then practice. You can use only the parts you need.

1
NotesKeep your study material
  1. Click Add Notes.
  2. Enter the title, subject, and your notes.
  3. Save the notes so they are available for studying and test creation.
2
ResearchTurn a topic into useful notes
  1. Choose the topic, subject, academic level, depth, review mode, and material.
  2. Click Build GPT Research Prompt, then use Copy & Open ChatGPT.
  3. Copy GPT's response, paste it on the same page, and click Save to My Notes.
3
TestsBuild, take, and review
  1. Enter the title, subject, study notes, assessment level, test type, test mode, and time limit.
  2. Set the number of questions, click Generate Exam Prompt, then use Copy & Open ChatGPT.
  3. Paste GPT's JSON into the same builder, click Import GPT JSON, review the questions, and save.
Recommended workflow: Research Topic → My Notes → My Tests → Take Test → Review Mistakes. For Korean, Japanese, Malay, and other content-heavy subjects, use Research Topic to organize wording, definitions, concepts, and discussions before generating questions.

Simple instructions

How to use StudyForge

You do not need to use every feature. Start with the part you need.

1

Notes

Put your reviewers, class notes, or study material here. Keep one topic organized in one place.

2

Research

Enter a topic, build the GPT prompt, open ChatGPT, then paste the useful notes back into StudyForge.

3

Tests

Choose your notes, set the test options, generate questions in ChatGPT, import the JSON, then save the test.

4

Flashcards

Use Flashcards to see a question first. Click Show Answer only when you are ready, then choose Again, Good, or Easy.

5

Active Recall

Read the question and answer it in your head before revealing the answer. Then mark whether you remembered it.

6

Mistake Bank

After tests, review the questions you missed. Use this when you want to focus on weak areas instead of reviewing everything.
